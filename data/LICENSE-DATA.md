## Privacy

Contributors consented to publication and are identified only by participant code
(P1, P2, P3). No names, dates of birth, contact details or location data are included.

## Note on the benchmark dataset

The Wisconsin Diagnostic Breast Cancer data is **not** redistributed here. It is loaded
directly from scikit-learn at runtime. See Street, Wolberg & Mangasarian (1993).
